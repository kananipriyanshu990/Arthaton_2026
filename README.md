# Artha Vardhan (अर्थ वर्धन)

> **Meaning:** "Wealth that Grows" (Sanskrit)  
> **Hackathon:** Arthaton 2026 | **Theme:** AI for Finance | **Format:** Solo

Artha Vardhan is an entirely passive, privacy-first, AI-assisted personal investment literacy and financial planning web application built for young Indians (students, freshers, and first jobbers).

---

## 🎯 Mission & Philosophy

- **Teach first, calculate second, plan third.**
- **No jargon without explanation.**
- **No real money, no live feeds, no legal risk:** Zero tracking or broker APIs.
- **Client-Side Storage Only:** 100% of user data lives in browser `localStorage`.
- **SEBI-Compliant:** Category-level educational guidance only; strictly avoids recommending individual AMC funds or stock picks.

---

## 🌈 The 8 Functional Zones & Accent Colors

1. **First Salary Onboarding (`#F5C518` / `#FFAA00`):** Interactive 50/30/20 paycheck routing and visual savings potential.
2. **Learn Zone (`#00D4FF`):** 8 comprehensive modules, 8 3D-flip Myth Busters, 28-term Jargon Dictionary with instant search, and 40 modular quiz questions with mastery badges.
3. **Calculators (`#F5C518` & `#00D4FF`):** Mathematically accurate SIP, Lumpsum, Dual-Mode CAGR, Inflation Power Loss, ELSS Tax Saver (Old vs New Regime), and What-If Simulator with side-by-side Recharts.
4. **Goal Planner (`#10B981`):** Multi-goal reverse SIP calculator supporting up to 10 goals with automatic keyword-based emoji tagging and progress bars.
5. **Portfolio Tracker (`#00D4FF` / `#EF4444`):** Manual investment ledger, CAGR calculations, future growth tables, allocation pie charts, and text summary exporter.
6. **Compounding Wow Screen (`#F5C518` on `#0D2B1D`):** Dramatic visual proof of early compounding and the "Cost of Waiting" 5-year interactive penalty calculation.
7. **Investment Roadmap (`#7C3AED`):** Vertical life-stage timeline highlighting the user's current age bracket from Foundation (18-22) to Preservation (50+).
8. **AI Risk Profiler & Recommender (`#00D4FF` & `#FFAA00`):** 10-question behavioral risk profiler that connects to the Google Gemini API to synthesize personality types and fund category allocations.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** React 18 with Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Custom Dark Navy `#0A0F2C` palette)
- **Charts:** Recharts
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **AI Integration:** Google AI Studio (Gemini 1.5 Flash API)
- **Data Persistence:** Browser `localStorage`
